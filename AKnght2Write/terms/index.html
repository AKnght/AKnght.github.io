On Google Play, it renews on its renewal date unless it is cancelled before then.

On Android: the Google Play Store app, your profile picture, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. The app’s Settings › Subscription screen links there too.   • Restoring. A subscription belongs to your store account, not to a login with us. On a new or reset device, Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases brings it back.

If a renewal cannot be confirmed because the device has been offline, the app keeps treating the subscription as active for up to 30 days before it goes back to the free terms.
