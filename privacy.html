Privacy

AKnght2Write collects nothing.

No account, no analytics, no server of ours. This page says what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and the little this website does.

Draft. AKnght2Write is still in development. This page describes the app as it is being built and will be made final before it reaches the stores.

The short version

AKnght2Write does not collect, transmit, sell or share any information about you or your writing. There are no accounts. We operate no servers for the app, so there is nowhere for your data to go and nothing for us to look at.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything below stays on your device, in the app’s own storage or in the folder you chose for your writing.

  • Your voice. The microphone is used only while you are dictating. Each recording is saved on the device so that it cannot be lost, turned into words on the device by a speech model that ships inside the app, and deleted once those words are on the page.
  • Your writing. Documents are plain text files in a folder on your device. If that folder is one that another service syncs (iCloud Drive, for example, or a folder you back up yourself), that service handles those files under its own terms. We never see them.
  • Your cast list and settings. The names you have taught the app, your preferences, and the count of words dictated this week are kept on the device.
  • Bluetooth buttons and headsets. A remote’s button presses are read as key presses, and a headset’s microphone is used only if you turn that on. Neither is recorded anywhere but the app’s settings.

The network

On Android, AKnght2Write does not request permission to use the internet at all. On every platform, the app itself makes no network calls.

If you subscribe, the purchase is made through Google Play or the App Store, under your account with that store and that store’s privacy policy. The app asks the store, on your device, whether a subscription is active. We receive no personal details from a purchase beyond the sales reports a store provides to every developer.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Children

AKnght2Write is a tool for writers and is not directed at children. Because it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from any third party. If you switch between the light and dark theme, that choice is remembered in your own browser and never leaves it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log the IP addresses of visitors in order to serve and secure the site, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you write to us, we use your email address to reply and for nothing else.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date. Questions are welcome at admin@aknghtstudios.com.
