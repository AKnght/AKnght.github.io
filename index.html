AKnght2Write

A writing tool that listens, and keeps it to itself.

Most dictation sends your voice to somebody else’s computer. AKnght2Write does its listening on the phone in your hand, so a first draft stays where first drafts belong: with you.

Nothing leaves the phone

Speech recognition runs on the device. No account, no cloud, no server of ours. On Android the app does not even ask for internet access.

It learns the names in your world

Fix Amira to Amyra once and it offers to remember. The cast list builds itself as you write, one book at a time.

Dialogue comes out as dialogue

Curly quotation marks, a new paragraph for each speaker, and quote that for the line it missed.

Edit by voice, safely

A small, fixed set of spoken commands. If it does not recognize one, it writes the words down rather than guessing. Your words are never lost.

Made for a pocket

Click a Bluetooth remote to start and to send. A buzz and a tone tell you it heard. The screen is optional, so the book keeps moving while you walk, drive, or lie in bed.

Hear it back

Listen to the chapter you just dictated, then fix it by voice. Dictate, listen, revise: the whole loop happens on the device.

The studio

There’s no I in AKnight.

Look closely at the name. AKnght Studios is a small team that has always worked remotely, making games and software together for more than a decade. We started with games: for kids, for phones, for other people’s ideas as well as our own. Now we make tools for writers, beginning with the one we wanted for ourselves.

No I

There is no I within our company, because only together can we succeed. Together we can make great things. As a team, we get things done.

Have fun

We love having fun, and think our work should show it. It is important to be professional, but fun is what sparks creativity and keeps us going.

Do your best

We expect the best from each other. However good or bad you are at a thing, always do your best. As a team, we deliver the best work we can.
